Try these study steps

  1. Choose one task and set a small goal.
  2. Gather the notes or materials you need.
  3. Take a short break between focused sessions.

Keep the plan balanced

  • Start with the nearest deadline.
  • Leave room for review and rest.
  • Adjust the plan when priorities change.
